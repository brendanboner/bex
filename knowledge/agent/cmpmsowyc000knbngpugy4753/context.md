### Environment Context
- **EP1 Network**: A wireless network managed through ExtremeCloud IQ, consisting of multiple access points and clients.
- **Key Metrics**:
  - **Inactive Radios**: Radios that are not broadcasting or receiving signals.
  - **Noise Floor**: Background noise levels that can interfere with wireless communication.
  - **Channel Congestion**: High utilization of wireless channels leading to degraded performance.
- **Systems**:
  - ExtremeCloud IQ: The primary platform for managing and monitoring the EP1 network.
  - Alerting System: Receives reports and alerts generated by this agent.
- **Thresholds**:
  - Noise Floor: Acceptable levels vary by environment but typically should not exceed -85 dBm.
  - Channel Utilization: Should remain below 80% for optimal performance.

### Key Facts
- The agent has read-only access to network data and cannot make configuration changes.
- Reports are sent to the designated alerting system or administrator for action.
